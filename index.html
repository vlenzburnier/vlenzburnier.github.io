Vicente Lenz Burnier

[I am a PhD candidate at TUDelft, in the applied probabilities and mathematical physics groups under the supervision of Elena Pulvirenti and Johan Dubbeldam. My research is currently focused on Metastability phenomenons over random graphs. Other research interests are Spectral theory and scattering theory for self-adjoint operators. I pursued a bachelor and master in pure mathematics at universidad de Chile, the later under the supervision of Giuseppe de Nittis from Universidad Católica de Chile. During it we studied the spectral and scattering properties of the 1-dimensional thermal Hamiltonian. Other interests of mine are ceramics, origami, fungal life, science-fiction and horror. ]
